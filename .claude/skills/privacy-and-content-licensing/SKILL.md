# Privacy and content licensing

- Show only headline, short excerpt or neutral summary, source, canonical link. Respect robots/RSS terms; keep per-source licensing_notes.
- Anonymous use; minimal data; opt-in analytics; account/data deletion (`DELETE /me`).
- Takedown/contact flow; flagged articles leave the feed immediately; preserve status history.
- Review: paywalled content never fully republished; policy_violation rejection path.
