# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

## The short version
- There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- We, the developers, collect no data about you. The app has no server of ours behind it.
- Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

## What the app does on your phone
- When you open the app, it asks each news source for its latest headlines (their public feeds). Those publishers can see your phone's internet address and the app's name, as with any internet request. We cannot see or control their logs; please read each publisher's own privacy policy.
- It stores on your phone: the headlines and short excerpts it fetched, the stories you saved, your settings, and the stories you hid or reported. None of this is sent anywhere.
- Android's cloud backup is switched off for this app, so this information is not copied to your Google account. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- When you tap "Read at <source>", your browser opens the publisher's page. When you share a story, the share sheet of your phone opens. Those apps and sites have their own policies.

## What the app shows
Only a headline, a short excerpt, the source and a link to the original story. The full story is always on the publisher's own site.

## Your choices
- Hide or report any story: it will not appear again on your phone, and you can bring it back under Settings, Hidden stories.
- Remove saved stories at any time.
- Turn any source off in Settings.
- Delete everything the app stores: Android Settings, Apps, Good News, Storage, Clear storage, or uninstall.

## How stories are chosen
Stories are chosen automatically from the headline and excerpt by published rules, on your phone. No person checks each story and the app cannot verify facts. Reports you make only affect your own phone.

## Changes to this policy
If the app ever collects data, this policy will change first and the app's store listing will be updated to match.
