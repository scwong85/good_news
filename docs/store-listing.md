## Short description (80 characters max)
A calm daily briefing of credible, constructive news. No accounts, no ads.

## Full description
Good News gives you a small daily briefing of 5 to 10 credible stories about progress, solutions and things getting better. Not every story is happy, but every story is constructive and comes from a trusted publisher.

- A short, finite list each day: you can finish it.
- Every story links to the original publisher; we show only the headline, a short excerpt and the source.
- "Why this story was included" explains each pick in plain language.
- Hide or report anything that doesn't belong, and it stays hidden.
- Save stories to read later.
- Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Stories are chosen automatically by published rules. No person checks each story, and the app cannot verify facts. Always read the original source.

## Data safety answers (match the app and `app/test/policy_test.dart`)
- Data collected: **none**. Data shared: **none**.
- Data encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS only; cleartext is disabled).
- Data deletion: all data is on the device; clear storage or uninstall deletes it.
- Ads: no. Accounts: no. Analytics and crash reporting: none.
- Permissions: INTERNET only.
- Third parties: the app requests the public RSS feeds of the listed publishers directly from the phone (they can see a normal internet request); links open in the user's browser.
